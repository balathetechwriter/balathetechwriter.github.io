User guide End-user help Product: Microsoft Edge (desktop)

Adding personal information to your Microsoft Edge profile

When you save your personal information in Edge, the browser can fill names, addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses for you on web forms. This guide shows how to add, review, and manage that information.

Applies to

Microsoft Edge on Windows. Menus and buttons may be worded slightly differently on other platforms or in newer browser versions.

Before you start

  • Make sure Edge is updated to the latest version.
  • Sign in to your profile so your saved information stays in sync across your devices. Signing in is optional — you can save personal information without signing in.

Adding personal information

Use the Add Personal Info option to store a name, address, phone number, email, and other details in your profile. Edge then offers to fill these details when you fill in online forms.

To add personal information:

  1. Open Edge, and then go to Settings > Passwords and autofill.
    Tip: In the address bar, you can also enter edge://settings/passwords and press Enter.
  2. In the Personal Info section, click Add Personal Info.
    The Add Personal Info window opens.

The Add Personal Info window contains these fields:

FieldWhat to enter
Country/regionYour country or region. Select it from the drop-down list.
NameYour full name.
OrganizationThe organization you belong to. This is optional.
Street addressYour street address, including the house or building number.
CityYour city or town.
PIN codeYour postal code.
StateYour state or province.
PhoneYour contact number, including the country code if you fill in forms outside your region.
EmailYour email address.
  1. Fill in the fields.
  2. Click Add.

The personal information is saved to your profile and is offered to autofill when a form needs it.

Editing or deleting personal information

  1. Go to Settings > Passwords and autofill > Personal Info.
  2. Find the entry you want to change.
  3. Do one of the following:
    • To edit — click Edit, change the details, and then click Save.
    • To delete — click Delete, and then confirm the deletion.
Keep one entry per address

If you move frequently, keep a single current entry instead of several old ones. Duplicate entries make autofill suggestions confusing, because Edge cannot tell which one is current.

What happens next

  • When a website asks for details you have saved, Edge offers to fill them automatically. Click the suggestion to accept it.
  • If a form was already filled with the wrong entry, click the field and choose the correct suggestion from the list that appears.

Related: if autofill is not working as expected, see Troubleshooting autofill issues in Microsoft Edge.
