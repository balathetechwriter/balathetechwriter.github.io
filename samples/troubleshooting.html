Troubleshooting autofill issues in Microsoft Edge

Edge is not offering to fill in your saved personal information, or it is filling in the wrong details. Find the symptom below that matches what you see, and follow the fix in order.

Not sure which section applies to you? Start with Section 1 — Edge does not offer to save personal information, the most common cause, then continue down the list.

Before you begin

Restart Edge once. Several autofill issues clear on their own after a restart or after the browser updates.

1. Edge does not offer to save personal information

You fill in a form, but the "Save personal info?" prompt never appears.

Possible cause: the Save and fill personal info setting is turned off.

To resolve:

  1. In Edge, go to Settings > Passwords and autofill.
  2. Turn on Save and fill personal info.
  3. Return to the form and submit it again. Edge now offers to save the details.

If the setting is already on, check whether the website marks the field as sensitive (for example, a credit-card-like field). Some sites deliberately disable autofill, and Edge respects that choice.

2. Saved information does not fill in a form

Your saved address or phone number is stored, but the form stays empty when you click a field.

Possible cause: the website's form does not label its fields in a way Edge recognizes, or you clicked a field Edge does not associate with your saved entry.

To resolve:

  1. Click the empty field, and then look for the autofill suggestion drop-down at the bottom of the field.
  2. If a suggestion appears, click your saved entry to fill the form.
  3. If no suggestion appears, enter the details manually. Edge will not reliably autofill this particular site; this is usually a limitation of how the site built its form, not a problem with your saved information.
3. Edge fills in outdated or incorrect details

The form fills with an old address, an old phone number, or an entry you deleted.

Possible cause: multiple saved entries exist, or the entry was edited on another device and has not finished syncing.

To resolve:

  1. Go to Settings > Passwords and autofill > Personal Info.
  2. Review the list and remove duplicate or outdated entries.
  3. Confirm the entry you want to use is the only one for that address.
  4. If you use multiple devices, sign in to the same profile and wait a minute for sync to complete, then try the form again.
4. The Add Personal Info button is missing or unavailable

In Settings > Passwords and autofill > Personal Info, the Add Personal Info button is grayed out or absent.

Possible cause: your organization manages Edge through policy, or your profile is a child profile with parental controls.

To resolve:

  1. If Edge is managed by your work or school, contact your IT administrator. Saving personal information can be disabled by policy, and the setting cannot be changed from the browser.
  2. If you use a child profile, ask the family organizer to allow autofill for the profile.
5. Personal information does not sync across your devices

You saved an entry on your computer, but it is not available on your phone or tablet.

Possible cause: sync is turned off, or you are signed in to different profiles on each device.

To resolve:

  1. Confirm you are signed in to the same Microsoft profile on every device.
  2. On each device, check Settings > Profiles > Sync, and make sure sync is turned on for personal information.
  3. Wait a few minutes, then check again. Large amounts of saved data can take time to sync.

Still stuck?

If none of the fixes above help, contact support and include the details below so the issue can be investigated quickly:

Edge versionSettings > About Microsoft Edge
Device & OSFor example, "Windows 11 laptop"
StepsExactly what you did before the problem appeared
ScreenshotA capture of the form or error you see
